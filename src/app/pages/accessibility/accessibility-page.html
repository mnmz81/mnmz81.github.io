Standard

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA, the guidelines adopted by the Israeli standard IS 5568 (ת"י 5568).

What was done

  • Every page works with the keyboard alone, starts with a "Skip to content" link and shows a visible focus ring.
  • Pages use semantic headings, landmarks and labelled links, so screen readers can navigate them.
  • Images have text alternatives; decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Text contrast meets level AA in both the light and the dark theme.
  • Text can be zoomed to 200%, and the layout adapts to small screens.
  • Animations respect the "reduce motion" setting of your system.
  • Every page is checked automatically against WCAG 2.1 AA (axe-core) in both themes on each change.

Known limitations

I am not aware of parts of the site that are not accessible. If you find one, please tell me.
